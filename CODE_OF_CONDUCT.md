# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the AgentSync
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and respectful to other people
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct which could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
maintainers at **dipakkr.co@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter of any incident.
